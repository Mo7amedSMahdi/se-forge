# Clinic booking: requirements (sample for the kit's discovery trial)

## Goal
A small clinic wants patients to book appointments online instead of by phone.

## Users
- Patients book, see and cancel their own appointments.
- Receptionists see the day's schedule and can book or cancel for any patient.
- An administrator manages doctors and their working hours.

## Features (first release)
1. A patient signs up and logs in.
2. A patient sees the free slots of a doctor for a chosen day and books one.
3. A patient sees their upcoming appointments and can cancel one.
4. The receptionist sees today's schedule for all doctors.
5. The administrator adds doctors and sets their weekly working hours.

## Notes
- The clinic is in Amman; patients use Arabic and English.
- Appointments are 20 minutes.
- The screens attached show the appointment list and the booking form.
